Unit 3: Beam Deflection, Columns and Torsion - Formulas and Methods

Mechanics of Solids (UE25ME241A)

Part 1: Formula sheetPart 2: Methods

This page has two parts: the formula sheet (everything on one page, with symbols, units, limits and sign convention) and the methods (a numbered recipe for each type of numerical question, one fully solved example for each, and a table to choose the method). Every formula here appears in the Unit 3 slides, notes, assignments or question bank; the few that come from elsewhere are marked source flag. Examples cite the slide, notes page or question-bank number where they come from.

Units used throughout. Use one consistent set. Either N and mm (then EE in N/mm2^2, II in mm4^4, MM in N mm, 1 GPa = 1000 N/mm2^2), or kN and m (then EE in kN/m2^2, II in m4^4). Angles of twist and slopes are in radians.

Part 1: Formula sheet

A. Deflection of beams

Sign convention (C01 p10). xx to the right, yy (and vv) upward positive; slope and rotation positive counter-clockwise; bending moment positive when it compresses the upper fibres (sagging).

Elastic curve equation

EId2vdx2=M(x)EI\dfrac{d^{2}v}{dx^{2}}=M(x)

Slope

θ≈dvdx\theta\approx\dfrac{dv}{dx} (small angles)

Curvature

1r=MEI=d2vdx2\dfrac1r=\dfrac{M}{EI}=\dfrac{d^{2}v}{dx^{2}}

Flexural rigidity

EIEI (N mm2^2 or N m2^2)

Double integration. EI v′=∫M dx+C1EI\,v'=\int M\,dx+C_1 and EI vEI\,v​=∬M dx dx+C1x+C2=\iint M\,dx\,dx+C_1x+C_2. Boundary conditions: v=0v=0 at a pin or roller; v=0v=0 and v′=0v'=0 at a fixed end. Symmetry: v′=0v'=0 at mid-span for symmetric loading. With several portions, add continuity of slope and deflection at each joint.

Singularity (Macaulay) functions. ⟨x−a⟩n=(x−a)n\langle x-a\rangle^{n}=(x-a)^{n} for x≥ax\ge a, =0=0 for x<ax<a (n≥0n\ge0); ∫⟨x−a⟩ndx\int\langle x-a\rangle^{n}dx​=⟨x−a⟩n+1n+1=\dfrac{\langle x-a\rangle^{n+1}}{n+1}. Brackets: point load PP: −P⟨x−a⟩1-P\langle x-a\rangle^{1}; couple M0M_0: +M0⟨x−a⟩0+M_0\langle x-a\rangle^{0} (sense as in the problem); UDL ww starting at aa: −w2⟨x−a⟩2-\dfrac w2\langle x-a\rangle^{2}; a UDL that stops at bb: add +w2⟨x−b⟩2+\dfrac w2\langle x-b\rangle^{2}. B. Columns

Euler load

PcrP_{cr}​=π2EIminLe2=\dfrac{\pi^{2}EI_{min}}{L_e^{2}}

Euler stress

σcr\sigma_{cr}​=π2E(Le/k)2=\dfrac{\pi^{2}E}{(L_e/k)^{2}}

Slenderness ratio

λ=Lek\lambda=\dfrac{L_e}{k},   k=IminA\;k=\sqrt{\dfrac{I_{min}}{A}} (k=d/4k=d/4 for a solid circle)

Limiting slenderness

λlim=π2Eσc\lambda_{lim}=\sqrt{\dfrac{\pi^{2}E}{\sigma_c}}; Euler valid only if λ>λlim\lambda>\lambda_{lim}

Rankine-Gordon

PR=σcA1+aλ2P_R=\dfrac{\sigma_cA}{1+a\lambda^{2}},   1PR=1Pc+1PE\;\dfrac1{P_R}=\dfrac1{P_c}+\dfrac1{P_E},   a=σcπ2E\;a=\dfrac{\sigma_c}{\pi^{2}E}

Effective length LeL_e for actual length LL: pinned-pinned LL; fixed-free 2L2L; fixed-fixed 0.5L0.5L; fixed-pinned 0.7L0.7L (C06 p16) or L/2L/\sqrt2 (C08 p39). Safe load =P/F.S.=P/\text{F.S.}; design: Pcr=F.S.×P_{cr}=\text{F.S.}\times working load. Crushing load Pc=σcAP_c=\sigma_cA.

Rankine constants (C08 p12), use as printed: mild steel σc=320\sigma_c=320, a=1/7500a=1/7500; cast iron 550, 1/16001/1600; wrought iron 250, 1/90001/9000; timber 50, 1/7501/750 (N/mm2^2).

Classification (slides disagree): C06: long λ>50\lambda>50, short λ<50\lambda<50 (or L/d<15L/d<15); C08: short λ<32\lambda<32, long λ>120\lambda>120. Euler assumptions: homogeneous, isotropic, elastic; uniform section; straight and axially loaded; negligible self weight; failure by buckling alone.

Assumptions: homogeneous isotropic material; elastic; plane sections stay plane; radial lines stay radial; uniform twist. Shear stress varies linearly with rr: zero at the axis, maximum at the surface. A circle of radius ρ\rho in a solid shaft carries a fraction (ρ/R)4(\rho/R)^4 of the torque.

Shafts in series (several torques): internal torque in each portion = algebraic sum of the torques on one side of a cut; θtotal=∑TiLiGiJi\theta_{total}=\sum\dfrac{T_iL_i}{G_iJ_i} (signed sum).

Design (strength and stiffness): minimum dd from T=τallowZpT=\tau_{allow}Z_p and from θ≤θallow\theta\le\theta_{allow}; use the larger. Same-weight comparison: hollow shaft with Do2−Di2=d2D_o^2-D_i^2=d^2 has Jh/JsJ_h/J_s​=(Do2+Di2)/d2=(D_o^2+D_i^2)/d^2​>1>1.

Part 2: Methods

M1. Double integration for slope and deflection

Use when: one or two load portions, or a standard load on a simple beam; you want v(x)v(x) and θ(x)\theta(x) everywhere.

  1. Draw the beam; choose xx and the sign convention.
  2. Find the support reactions.
  3. Write M(x)M(x) for each portion (cut at every change of load).
  4. EI v′′=MEI\,v''=M; integrate twice, with C1,C2C_1,C_2 per portion.
  5. List conditions: supports (boundary), symmetry, and continuity of vv and v′v' at each joint.
  6. Solve the constants; evaluate vv and θ\theta where asked.

  2. AC: M=104xM=104x. CB: MM​=104x−156(x−1.5)=104x-156(x-1.5) (kN, m).
  3. AC: EIv′=52x2+C1EIv'=52x^2+C_1, EIvEIv​=17.33x3+C1x+C2=17.33x^3+C_1x+C_2. CB: EIv′EIv'​=52x2−78(x−1.5)2+C3=52x^2-78(x-1.5)^2+C_3, EIvEIv​=17.33x3−26(x−1.5)3+C3x+C4=17.33x^3-26(x-1.5)^3+C_3x+C_4.
  4. v=0v=0 at x=0x=0: C2=0C_2=0. Slope continuity at x=1.5x=1.5: C1=C3C_1=C_3. Deflection continuity: C4=0C_4=0. v=0v=0 at x=4.5x=4.5: C3=C1=−195C_3=C_1=-195. Step 5.

M2. Standard-case formulas

Use when: the beam and load match a row of the standard-case table (cantilever or simply supported; point load or UDL over the whole span); only the end or mid-span values are needed.

  1. Identify the case and check the load is at the position in the table (tip, mid-span, whole span).
  2. Put EE, II, LL, load in consistent units (EIEI in N mm2^2).
  3. Read θmax\theta_{max} and vmaxv_{max} from the table. State direction.

Step 2.

M3. Singularity (Macaulay) functions

Use when: many loads, point loads off-centre, couples or partial UDLs, or when you want to avoid continuity equations.

  1. Reactions.
  2. Write one M(x)M(x) with a bracket for each load; continue and cancel a UDL that stops early.
  3. EI v′′=M(x)EI\,v''=M(x); integrate twice using ∫⟨x−a⟩n\int\langle x-a\rangle^n​=⟨x−a⟩n+1n+1=\dfrac{\langle x-a\rangle^{n+1}}{n+1}; add C1C_1, C2C_2 once.
  4. Use v=0v=0 (and v′=0v'=0 at a fixed end) to find C1,C2C_1,C_2.
  5. Maximum deflection: v′=0v'=0, solve for xx, check the portion, evaluate vv.

M4. Euler buckling load

Use when: a long, slender column (λ>λlim\lambda>\lambda_{lim}) and the buckling load or a safe load is wanted.

  1. End conditions give LeL_e.
  2. AA, IminI_{min} (smaller of IxxI_{xx}, IyyI_{yy}), k=Imin/Ak=\sqrt{I_{min}/A}, λ=Le/k\lambda=L_e/k.
  3. λlim=π2E/σc\lambda_{lim}=\sqrt{\pi^2E/\sigma_c}; if λ>λlim\lambda>\lambda_{lim} Euler is valid.
  4. PcrP_{cr}​=π2EImin/Le2=\pi^2EI_{min}/L_e^2. Safe load =Pcr/F.S.=P_{cr}/\text{F.S.}

Example (QB Q10). Steel rod d=40d=40 mm, L=2000L=2000 mm, E=2×105E=2\times10^{5} N/mm2^2.

  1. II​=πd4/64=\pi d^4/64​=1.2566×105=1.2566\times10^{5} mm4^4.

  2. Pinned-pinned:

    Le=2000 mmL_e=2000\text{ mm}

    Pcr=π2(2×105)(1.2566×105)20002P_{cr} = \dfrac{\pi^2(2\times10^{5})(1.2566\times10^{5})}{2000^2}

    Pcr=62.01 kNP_{cr} = 62.01\text{ kN}

  3. Fixed-free: Le=4000L_e=4000 mm: Pcr=15.50P_{cr}=15.50 kN.

M5. Rankine-Gordon column design

Use when: short or intermediate columns (Euler not valid), or any column for a safe-load estimate; or a section must be found from a working load.

  1. LeL_e from end conditions; AA, IminI_{min}, kk.
  2. PR=σcA1+a(Le/k)2P_R=\dfrac{\sigma_cA}{1+a(L_e/k)^2} with σc\sigma_c and aa from the problem or the table (C08 p12).
  3. Safe load =PR/F.S.=P_R/\text{F.S.} For a design, set PR=F.S.×P_R=\text{F.S.}\times working load; if the section is unknown, express kk in terms of the diameter and solve the resulting quadratic.

M7. Shaft design for strength and stiffness

Use when: the diameter is wanted from power, an allowable stress and/or an allowable twist.

M8. Shafts with several portions (stepped, composite, pulleys)

Use when: torques are applied at more than one section, or the diameter, material or section changes.

  1. Split the shaft at every change of torque, diameter, GG or section.
  2. Internal torque of each portion: algebraic sum of external torques on one side of a cut (note opposite senses).
  3. For each portion: JiJ_i, then ϕi=TiLi/(GiJi)\phi_i=T_iL_i/(G_iJ_i) and τi=Tici/Ji\tau_i=T_ic_i/J_i.

Use when: a section must be chosen so that bending stress and deflection are both within limits.

  1. MmaxM_{max} (e.g. wL2/8wL^2/8); ZminZ_{min}​=Mmax/σallow=M_{max}/\sigma_{allow}.
